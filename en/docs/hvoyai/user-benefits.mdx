## Tips

If you are using hvoy for the first time, complete email verification to register your hvoy account and receive registration points immediately. You can then sign in daily to check in and accumulate points for benefits.

Prize inventory and activity rules for free lotteries, free API Keys, and free invite codes may change over time. The exact claimable items and requirements are shown on each benefit activity page.
